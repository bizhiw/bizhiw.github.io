Weaving Our Shared Stories

Our marriage brings together everything that made us. Alborz carries his Persian heritage; Neecha carries hers, Anishinaabe and Celtic. Rather than choose between them, we are weaving them together.

A traditional Persian ceremony

We will marry in the Persian tradition, before a sofreh aghd, the beautiful spread laid out with meaning in every object upon it. Ours will hold pieces of both our families and our story, so that the things we come from sit side by side as we begin something new.

On the first day of spring

Our wedding falls on Nowruz, the Persian New Year and the spring equinox, when the world tips toward light and everything begins again. It feels right to start our life together on a day the whole world is being reborn, and we would love for you to share the budding of this new season with us.

Parking

Complimentary parking is available on site, so feel free to drive yourself and leave the car with us for the evening.

Accessibility

The venue has accessibility features throughout. If there's anything in particular we can arrange to make your evening easier, tell us on your RSVP and we'll take care of it.

Food & drink

No one will go hungry. The evening includes an open bar, passed canapés, a three-course dinner and late night snacks to carry you through the dancing.

Coat check

There's a coat check and a space to leave your coats and bags, so you can arrive bundled up and dance unburdened.

Indoors

The ceremony and reception are both held indoors, in the main hall.

Dress code

Creative formal, black tie optional. We encourage moody drama and flair, rich colour and your most beautiful self. A dark suit or tuxedo, a floor-length gown or an elegant cocktail dress are all welcome.
