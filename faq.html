Questions

What time should I arrive?

Arrival and welcome drinks begin at 3:30 pm, with the ceremony at 4:00 pm. Please plan to be seated a few minutes beforehand.

What is the dress code?

Creative formal, black tie optional. We encourage moody drama and flair, rich colour and your most beautiful self. A dark suit or tuxedo, a floor-length gown or an elegant cocktail dress are all welcome. If you’d like a little guidance, here’s a lovely guide.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Of course, with one gentle wish. We'd love for your plus-one to be someone who truly holds a place in your life, a long-term partner rather than a brand-new or passing romance. Our day is small and dear to us, and we'd rather fill the room with the people who already mean something to you. If you'd like to bring a guest, note them on the RSVP form, and if you're ever unsure, just reach out and we'll happily sort it out together.

Are children invited?

Any children included in your invitation are named on it. For families with little ones, we'll have a private, secluded room set aside for new parents, a quiet space for infant care, feeding and a moment's rest whenever you need it.

Is there parking?

Yes, complimentary parking is available on site. Feel free to drive yourself and leave the car with us for the evening.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes, the venue has accessibility features throughout. If there's anything specific we can arrange to make your evening easier, let us know on your RSVP and we'll take care of it.

Is the ceremony unplugged?

We kindly ask guests to put phones and cameras away during the ceremony so everyone can be fully present. Our photographer will capture it all.

Are the ceremony and reception indoors?

Yes, both are held indoors in the main hall at Vaughan Estate.

Will there be food and drink?

The evening includes a full open bar, passed canapés, a three-course dinner, and late night snacks to carry you through the dancing.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

There’s a place to tell us about restrictions and allergies on the RSVP form. We’ll pass everything along to the caterer.

Is there a coat check?

Yes, there's a coat check and a space to leave coats and bags, so you can arrive bundled up against the March air and dance unburdened.

How do I RSVP?

The RSVP form is open now on the RSVP page, and you can resubmit any time to update your answer. We'll confirm the reply-by date soon, but the sooner you let us know, the better, thank you.

Can I change my RSVP?

Yes. Just submit the RSVP form again with your updated answers before the deadline.

Where should I stay?

A few of our favourite hotels are in Yorkville, centrally located and only a short drive from the venue. See the Travel page for the ones we'd suggest.

Is transportation provided?

We aren't arranging transport, the venue is right in midtown and easy to reach. A short drive or ride share will bring you to the door, and there's complimentary parking on site.

What time does the reception end?

Dancing carries on until 1:00 am, so there’s plenty of evening to enjoy.

Are there other events besides the wedding day?

A few things, such as a welcome party, are in the works. Details for any additional events will reach you privately, by email or text, once you've RSVP'd and shared your contact information with us, so be sure to include it on your reply.

Is there a registry? What about gifts?

Your presence is truly the greatest gift. In keeping with Persian tradition we aren’t holding a registry; gifts are given as cash. There will be a gifting and photo ceremony during the celebration, with a cash box set aside for the evening. If this custom is new to you, here's a lovely guide to it.

Who should I contact with questions?

Reach out any time, our email is in the footer below.

For anything at all, get in touch, we’re happy to help.
