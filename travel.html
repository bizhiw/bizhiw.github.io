Where to stay

A few hotels we'd happily point you to, all an easy ride to the venue. We haven't set aside room blocks, but if enough of you reach out and let us know you're coming, we're glad to look into a group discount, so do send us a note.

The Anndore House

15 Charles Street East, Toronto

A characterful boutique hotel steps from Yonge and Bloor, with a lively bar and a moody, design-led feel, at friendlier rates than the big Yorkville names.

Getting here

The venue sits on the Estates of Sunnybrook grounds, tucked past the hospital and the Medical Science buildings, right down at the end of Armistice Drive by the Memorial Arboretum and the Glendon forest. It's wonderfully easy to reach from every part of the city.

By car

Turn off Bayview Avenue into the Sunnybrook grounds and follow the road all the way down Armistice Drive, past the hospital and Medical Science buildings, to the Estates of Sunnybrook at the very end by the Arboretum. There's complimentary parking on site. Ride shares drop right at the door.

By subway & bus

Take Line 1 (Yonge) to Lawrence Station, then hop on the 124 Sunnybrook bus, which runs along Lawrence and Bayview right into the Sunnybrook grounds. From there it's a short, pretty walk down to the Estates.

Coming from out of town

Toronto Pearson (YYZ) is roughly 30 to 40 minutes away by car, and Union Station downtown connects VIA Rail and GO Transit, a straightforward ride up to midtown.
